SHIVAJI MAHARAJ


Information about Shivaji Maharaj

  1. His birth place & date

    • Shivaji Maharaj was born on 19 February 1630.
    • His birth place was Shivneri Fort, Junnar in present day, Maharashtra
    • His father was Sahaji Bhosle and mother was Jijabai

  2. Tranning

    • Shivaji Maharaj recived millitary tranning under Dadoji Konddev.
    • He was trained in:
    • Sword Fighting
    • Horse Ridding
    • Archery
    • Guerrilla warfare
    • Fort Mangement
    • He also learn geography of the Sahyadri mountains, which later helped him in warfare.

  3. Top three battles

    • (a) Battle of Pratapghad (1659)


    • Faught between Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and the Bijapur genral Afzal Khan.
    • Took place near Pratapgad fort.
    • Shivaji Maharaj used smart military statergy and defete Afzal Khan.
    • This victory increase Shivaji Maharaj fame and strength in Maharashtra.

    • (b) Battle of Pawan Kindh (1660)


    • Faught against the forces of Bijapur Sultanaet.
    • Baji Prabhu Deshpande played a heroic role in protecting Shivaji Maharaj during his escape from Panhala Fort.
    • The battle showed the bravery and the loyalty of Maratha soldiers.
    • It become the most important events in Maratha history.

    • (c) Battle of Singhgad (1670)


    • Faught to capture Singad fort from Mughals.
    • Led by the brave Maratha commander Tanhaji Malusare.
    • Tanhaji lost his life during this battle.
    • Shivaji Maharaj said,"the fort is win, but lion is lost.

  4. Coronation Ceremony

    • Chatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was crowned as chatrapati on 6 June 1674
    • The coronation took place in Raigad Fort.
    • The ceremony was performed as the hindu tradition.
    • The coronation mark the establishment of Swarajya.
